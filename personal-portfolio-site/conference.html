Day of Data Detroit

A free community conference connecting Southeast Michigan professionals and students around data engineering, analytics, Microsoft Fabric, SQL, Power BI, Azure, AI and modern data platforms.

Official event pageVolunteerSponsor the event
Community-first
Free to attend

Designed to expand access to practical technical education and strengthen the regional data community.

Audience: engineers, architects, analysts, students, faculty, leaders and career changers.

Technical learning

Sessions and demos across SQL Server, Azure SQL, Microsoft Fabric, Power BI, Real-Time Intelligence, AI, data engineering, governance and complementary modern data platforms.

Community connection

Meet speakers, peers, employers, students, universities and local technology communities in a welcoming regional setting.

Volunteer powered

The event depends on community volunteers, venue partners, speakers, sponsors and in-kind support.
