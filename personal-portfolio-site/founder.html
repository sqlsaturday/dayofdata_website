Founder

Mou Rakshit

Data, AI and analytics leader, community organizer, speaker and educator focused on building practical learning opportunities across Southeast Michigan.

Mou founded Day of Data Detroit, the Detroit Southeast Michigan Databricks User Group and the Southeast Michigan Data & AI Community to connect professionals, students, universities and employers around accessible technical education.

Her public speaking and teaching cover Microsoft Fabric, Real-Time Intelligence, Power BI, SQL, Azure data, Databricks, governance, conversational BI and enterprise AI.

Leadership model

Founder-led, community-built.

The goal is not to make the organization dependent on one person. Mou serves as founder and lead organizer while the community recruits volunteers, program leads, sponsors, university partners and a broader steering structure.
