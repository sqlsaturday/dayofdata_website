Community partnerships

Help build a stronger Southeast Michigan data ecosystem.

We are building long-term relationships with universities, student groups, employers, libraries, technology communities, nonprofits and venue partners.

Universities & students

Share events with students, host a meetup, nominate student volunteers, provide classrooms or introduce faculty and technical speakers.

Employers & venues

Host an evening or Saturday meetup, provide meeting space, refreshments, speakers, volunteers or practical case studies.

Community organizations

Cross-promote events, collaborate on programming, share speakers and help reach underserved or emerging technology communities.

Regional focus
Detroit, Ann Arbor, Northville, Novi, Plymouth, Canton, Dearborn, Southfield, Troy, Rochester and the broader Southeast Michigan region.
