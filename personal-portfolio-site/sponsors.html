Sponsor value

Visibility tied to useful community contribution.

Recognition is designed to be proportional to the support provided and the program being sponsored. Exact benefits are confirmed in writing for each partnership.

Digital recognition

Logo/name recognition on applicable community or event pages, sponsor sections and selected event communications.

Event presence

Appropriate acknowledgement during the supported event, with additional participation options where relevant to the program.

Community connection

Credible association with free technical education, student access, regional professional development and community building.

Legal and payment transparency: this site does not claim registered nonprofit or tax-exempt status unless and until that status is formally established. Every sponsorship or support arrangement will identify the applicable payment entity and whether any contribution is tax-deductible before funds are accepted.
